# Calibration Protocol v0.2

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE TARGET-OUTCOME CODING**

This document incorporates the mandatory revisions from referee review.

## 1. Observational unit

The unit of analysis is the **sale-household**, never the individual photograph.

Photographs are evidence from a variable-fidelity sensor network generated by the sale company.

## 2. Outcome vocabulary

For target object `j` in household `i`, use only:

- `OBSERVED`
- `NOT_OBSERVED_GIVEN_OPPORTUNITY`
- `INDETERMINATE`

Do not use `ABSENT`.

## 3. General detection-opportunity rule

Set object-specific opportunity to `YES` only when photographs expose the physical locations in which the object would ordinarily be stored or used.

Opportunity must be triggered by **spatial coverage**, not by the presence of a target-adjacent valuable object.

## 4. Coverage classes

- `C0`: unusable; selected items only or no meaningful spatial context.
- `C1`: weak; mostly close-ups, substantial household areas unseen.
- `C2`: moderate; multiple rooms/storage areas visible, but meaningful selection remains.
- `C3`: strong; broad room-level documentation across much of the household.

## 5. VHS

### Observed

At least one identifiable VHS cassette, VHS case, labeled VHS storage container, or unmistakable group of VHS tapes.

### Opportunity

At least one of:

- >=75% of a visible entertainment/media shelving unit is exposed;
- >=75% of a bookcase or shelving wall within a living/media room is exposed;
- substantial contents of a dedicated media cabinet are visible;
- multiple storage shelves in a basement/den/family room are shown at sufficient resolution to distinguish VHS-sized objects.

A television alone does not create VHS opportunity. A close-up of one DVD shelf does not create VHS opportunity unless surrounding storage is also visible.

## 6. VCR

### Observed

Identifiable VCR, VHS/DVD combination deck, or TV/VCR combination. Preserve subtype.

### Opportunity

At least one of:

- principal TV/media installation is visible, including >=75% of associated equipment shelves;
- electronics/storage area exposes multiple components at sufficient resolution to distinguish component types;
- substantial shelving/storage in a den, basement, or media room is visible.

VCR and VHS opportunity are independent variables.

## 7. Books

### Abundance

- `B0`: none observed
- `B1`: 1–9
- `B2`: approximately 10–49
- `B3`: approximately 50–199
- `B4`: >=200
- `BX`: cannot reasonably estimate

Primary binary book outcome: substantial household book presence = `B2–B4`.

### Opportunity

At least one of:

- substantial wall coverage in two or more principal living spaces;
- >=75% exposure of visible bookcases/shelving in a principal living space;
- broad documentation of living room plus den/study/home office;
- `C3` whole-house coverage clearly documenting normal shelf-bearing spaces.

## 8. Negative controls

Use a scale gradient:

- bed — large-object control
- lamp — common room-scale control
- microwave — medium-object control

Landline telephone is explicitly excluded as a negative control because its true prevalence declined during the study period.

### Microwave opportunity

`YES` if photographs expose >=75% of major kitchen countertop area plus the principal range/refrigerator/cabinet wall, or otherwise clearly document the expected built-in location.

## 9. Collector state

Do not exclude collectors from the prevalence population.

Code positive observations where supportable as:

- `U`: ordinary utility context
- `R`: residual/obsolete retained context
- `C`: deliberate collecting/display context
- `X`: indeterminate

This permits separate estimation of total material survival and ordinary/residual survival.

## 10. Reliability gates

At least 25% of calibration households must be independently double-coded.

### Object coding

Require Cohen's kappa >= 0.70 or Gwet's AC1 >= 0.75.

### Detection opportunity

Require Cohen's kappa >= 0.70 or Gwet's AC1 >= 0.75.

### Coverage C0–C3

Use weighted kappa. Desired >= 0.70.

### Hard stop

If coverage or detection-opportunity reliability is < 0.60, temporal inference stops. Revise the codebook without examining temporal target effects, then recode.

## 11. Cohort firewall

The allowed estimand is:

`P(object retained | liquidation year)` among archived estate-liquidated households.

The following interpretation is forbidden without external calibration:

`P(object owned | general contemporary household in year)`.

Observable cohort context may be retained descriptively, but birth cohort must not be inferred from liquidation year.

## 12. Failure rules

### Potentially fatal

1. Denominator failure: coverage or detection-opportunity reliability < 0.60.
2. Negative-control collapse: beds, lamps, or microwaves show unexplained temporal disappearance comparable to target categories.

### Major redesign triggers

3. Company confounding: major apparent temporal trends disappear under company adjustment or within-company restriction.
4. High-coverage reversal: major trends reverse when restricted to C3/high-opportunity households.

## 13. Calibration firewall

The 20-household calibration corpus is for:

- archival image-survival testing;
- gallery-completeness auditing;
- codebook refinement that does not inspect temporal target effects;
- coverage/opportunity reliability;
- ingestion engineering.

Do **not** calculate prevalence curves, change points, regional effects, or extinction dates from the calibration outcomes.

## 14. Ingestion gate

Before visual coding, each sale should have an auditable gallery manifest containing:

- source sale ID and URL;
- extraction method;
- raw picture-array count;
- unique picture count after URL deduplication;
- ordered picture URLs;
- extraction time;
- error state;
- later, an independent completeness cross-check where available.

Raw household images are not committed to the public Git repository.
